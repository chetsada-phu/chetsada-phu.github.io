Assessment project · Retail

Retail sales forecasting

Forecasting 90 days of sales for 500 store-item series using 913,000 historical records.

5.939 mean validation MAELightGBM + seasonal blend, compared with 6.152 for standalone LightGBM.
LightGBMRidgeSeasonalityTemporal validation
Methods, results, and limitations

Compared six approaches across eight expanding-window origins. Calendar, lag, and shifted rolling features were checked for future-sales leakage. The fixed 50:50 ensemble reduced mean validation MAE by 3.46% and generated 45,000 final forecasts.

Validation was reused during development, and the 2017 comparison is diagnostic. Final forecast actuals are unavailable. The target is observed sales, which may differ from unconstrained demand.

Academic project · Financial time series

USD/THB forecasting

Comparing univariate and multivariate forecasts with exchange-rate history, rate and inflation differentials, Brent prices, and VIX.

A strong baseline is hard to beat.The validation-selected RNN did not outperform Random Walk on Test RMSE.
PyTorchXGBoostChronosTimesFM
Methods, results, and limitations

Benchmarked seven approaches: Random Walk, XGBoost, RNN, LSTM, Transformer, Chronos, and TimesFM. Used 501-observation rolling histories, 103 validation forecasts, and 85 test forecasts. Hyperparameters were selected on Validation RMSE and fixed during Test.

The revised test period had been inspected previously. Macro publication timing and foundation-model pretraining overlap are unverified. These results do not establish trading performance.
